4-Phase Speedcubing Blueprint

Master the Fridrich (CFOP) method from Cross to Permutation

1

Phase 1: The White Cross (EO Alignment)

Target Move Budget: ≤ 8 moves
Intuitive Phase

Orient 4 white edge pieces around the white center piece while ensuring side colors match adjacent Red, Blue, Orange, and Green centers. In group theory, this restricts the search tree to states where bottom edge orientations are invariant.

Key Triggers: F R' D' R F2 or intuitive bottom face insertions
2

Phase 2: First Two Layers (F2L)

41 Standard Algorithms / Slot Pairing
Efficiency Core

Solve the 4 corner-edge slots simultaneously without disturbing the white cross. Pair a corner piece with its matching edge in the top layer and insert both into their bottom slot in a single fluid motion.

Standard Pair Insertion: U R U' R' (Right) • U' L' U L (Left)
3

Phase 3: Orientation of Last Layer (OLL)

2-Look OLL / 57 Full Cases
Yellow Face Complete

Orient all 9 yellow facelets facing upwards on the top layer. 2-Look OLL breaks this down into two sub-steps: (1) Form a yellow top cross via F R U R' U' F', then (2) Orient the remaining 4 corners using Sune (R U R' U R U2 R').

Yellow Edge Cross: F (R U R' U') F'
4

Phase 4: Permutation of Last Layer (PLL)

2-Look PLL / 21 Full Permutations
Final Solved State

Permute all pieces on the top layer into their solved target locations while preserving orientation. 2-Look PLL first solves the 4 corner positions with T-Perm or Y-Perm, then cycles the 3/4 edge pieces with U-Perm or H-Perm to reach the identity permutation $\{1\}$.
