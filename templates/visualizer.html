Mathematical Foundation of Permutation Networks

1. The Group Order & Structure

The Rubik's cube group $G$ has order:
|G| = (8! × 3⁷) × (12! × 2¹¹) / 2 = 43,252,003,274,489,856,000
Representing permutations of 8 corner cubies and 12 edge cubies under parity and orientation constraints.

2. Two-Phase Algorithm Subgroups (Kociemba)

Kociemba's algorithm reduces the problem through a nested subgroup chain:
G₀ = ⟨U, D, L, R, F, B⟩ (All 4.3 × 10¹⁹ states)
↓ Phase 1 (Orientation invariant)
G₁ = ⟨U, D, L2, R2, F2, B2⟩ (Subgroup of order ~1.95 × 10¹²)
↓ Phase 2 (Permutation reduction)
G₂ = { Identity / Solved }

3. Permutation Cycles in Visualizer

Every face turn is an element of the symmetric group $S_{54}$ consisting of two 4-cycles on the face stickers and four 3-cycles on adjacent facelet edges. The circular orbit graph visually maps these permutations in real-time.
