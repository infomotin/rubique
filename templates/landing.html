Abstract Algebra

The Group of the Rubik's Cube

The Rubik's cube group $G$ is generated by 6 face turns $\langle U, D, L, R, F, B \rangle$. It is isomorphic to a semi-direct product of abelian orientation groups with the alternating and symmetric groups:

$$G \cong (\mathbb{Z}_3^7 \times \mathbb{Z}_2^{11}) \rtimes (A_8 \times M_{12})$$
Total distinct states: 43,252,003,274,489,856,000
  • Corner Orientation Invariant: $\sum_{i=1}^8 x_i \equiv 0 \pmod 3$
  • Edge Orientation Invariant: $\sum_{i=1}^{12} y_i \equiv 0 \pmod 2$
  • Permutation Parity: $\text{sgn}(\text{corners}) = \text{sgn}(\text{edges})$
Phase 1: $G_0 \to G_1$

Edge and corner orientations are preserved. Restricts available moves to $\langle U, D, L2, R2, F2, B2 \rangle$.

Phase 2: $G_1 \to G_2$

Permutes corners and edges within subgroups, resolving the state to the identity element $\{1\}$.
